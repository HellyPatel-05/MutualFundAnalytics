AMFI Scheme Codes

AMFI (Association of Mutual Funds in India) assigns a unique AMFI code to each mutual fund scheme.

These codes act as unique identifiers and help in tracking, managing, and linking mutual fund data across different datasets.

In this project, AMFI codes are used to connect information between the fund_master dataset and the nav_history dataset.

Using AMFI codes ensures that NAV records, fund details, and scheme information correspond to the correct mutual fund scheme.
